1. Who we are

Vault Blitz is developed and operated by Marcus Warnsley, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at idreamofcode@gmail.com.

2. Summary

3. Information saved on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device: your gold balance, tools, campaign stars and progress, saved attempts, reward records, Academy progress and your settings (such as sound volume and board theme). This information is not sent to us.

  • Backups. Vault Blitz stores game progress and settings locally and does not provide its own cloud-save synchronization. On Android, eligible app data may be backed up or transferred by the operating system, depending on your device and backup settings. Restoration is not guaranteed, and uninstalling or resetting may erase progress. We cannot see, recover or restore your progress. On iOS, we have not verified whether system backups include the app's saved data.
  • Reset. Settings → Intel → Reset Local Progress returns your progress to a new player's, and keeps your settings.
  • Delete. Settings → Intel → Delete Account removes everything the app stores on this device, including settings. In the test builds there is no online account, so this action only affects your device.

Neither reset nor delete removes crash and error reports that were already sent (see Retention and deletion).

4. Crash and error diagnostics (Sentry)

Test builds use Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to report crashes and unexpected errors to us. A report is created only when the app crashes or hits an error; the app does not send reports about normal play.

What a report contains

  • The type of error and where in the app's code it happened (stack trace file names, function names and line numbers). Free-text error messages are replaced with a generic placeholder before sending.
  • The app's identity and version: bundle identifier, version, build number, the environment (“testing”), platform and the Sentry software version.
  • A report ID and the time of the error.
  • Up to 20 recent events from a fixed list, to show what led to the error: which screen was open, whether the app was in the foreground or background, and the name and outcome of a game operation (for example saving progress, starting an attempt or using a tool). These can include a campaign vault name, an attempt number such as “attempt-3”, a reward identifier, a tool type or the kind of save involved.

What the app removes or does not collect

The app is configured not to attach your name, email, a player or account ID, the device name, user or device details, your gold balance, save files, text you type, screenshots, screen recordings, the screen layout, network requests or console logs. Session tracking, performance monitoring and session replay are turned off.

IP address and approximate location

Like any internet service, Sentry receives the device's IP address when a report is sent to it. This applies only to devices that send a crash or error report, not to everyone who plays. We have set up our Sentry project so that IP addresses are not stored with error reports and the approximate location Sentry would otherwise derive from them (such as a country or city) is removed. Some earlier test reports, sent before these settings were in place, include an approximate location; they are kept and deleted as described in Retention and deletion.

Delivery

If the device is offline, a small number of reports (up to 20) can wait on the device and be sent the next time the app runs with a connection.

Why and who

We use these reports only to diagnose and fix problems in the app. Sentry processes them on our behalf as a service provider, on servers in the United States. Sentry's handling is described in the Sentry Privacy Policy.

5. Test programs (TestFlight and Google Play)

The app is currently distributed only to invited testers through Apple TestFlight and Google Play internal testing. These programs are run by Apple and Google under their own terms and privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

  • To invite you, we give Apple or Google the email address you provide for testing. It is stored in App Store Connect or Google Play Console.
  • If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shows us your comments, any screenshot you attach, and the device and app details Apple includes. If you were invited by email, your email address appears with your feedback. If the app crashes on iOS, Apple may also provide crash reports through TestFlight.
  • Google Play may provide us with crash and performance information about the test build according to your device settings and Google's policies.

6. Support email

If you email idreamofcode@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as screenshots or device details. We use this only to reply and to fix the problems you report. The inbox is provided by Google (Gmail). Please do not send passwords, access tokens or payment-card details.

7. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No gameplay analytics service.
  • No account sign-in in the test builds.
  • No access to your device's location services (such as GPS), contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The app plays music and sound effects only. How crash and error reports handle IP addresses and approximate location is described in section 4.
  • No real-money purchases in the test builds.
  • We do not sell or rent information about you.

The app's code contains online features (see Features that are not active). They are switched off in the test builds, which are configured with no online game server, so those features make no network connections.

8. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added cookies, analytics, tracking or external fonts. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to other sites, such as Apple, Google and Sentry, are governed by those sites' own policies.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: kept until you reset, delete or uninstall (see section 3).
  • Crash and error reports: Sentry keeps individual error events for a period that depends on our Sentry plan: 30 days on the free Developer plan, and 90 days on the Team and Business plans. Our account is currently on a Business plan trial, and we intend to move to the Developer plan when the trial ends. The period that applies is set by Sentry for our plan, so it can change when our plan changes. Once an event expires it can no longer be viewed or restored, but Sentry may keep related records, such as the grouped issue and its total event count. We can also delete reports in Sentry.
  • Test feedback and invitations: kept in App Store Connect and Google Play Console under Apple's and Google's retention rules. We can remove a tester from a test.
  • Support email: we keep your emails and our replies for as long as we need them to answer your request, follow up on the problem you reported, and meet any legal obligations that apply to us. You can ask us to delete your correspondence, and we will do so unless we need to keep it for one of those reasons.

Crash reports contain no name, email or player ID, so we usually cannot tell which reports came from you. If you ask us to delete them, telling us your device, app build and the approximate time of a crash helps us look.

10. Security

Crash and error reports are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the Sentry project, the testing consoles and the support inbox is limited to the people working on Vault Blitz. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Where information is processed

Sentry stores our crash and error reports in the United States. Apple, Google and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries, as described in their policies. If you live elsewhere, your information may be processed in a country with different data-protection rules from yours.

12. Children

Vault Blitz is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not ask for your age. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us so we can delete it.

13. Your choices and requests

  • Reset or delete your local data from the app's Settings (see section 3).
  • Stop sending crash reports by uninstalling the app or leaving the test.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing idreamofcode@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

14. Features that are not active

The app's code includes features that are switched off in the current test builds: online bounties and raids, which would use an online game server (Supabase) and an anonymous guest account; publishing vault designs; and a store for real-money gold packs. None of these are active, and the in-app gold store has no real payment system.

Before we turn on online features, real-money purchases, analytics or any other new collection of information, we will update this policy to describe what is collected and who processes it, and update the app-store privacy disclosures to match.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will also take reasonable steps to let testers know, for example in the test program's release notes.

16. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: idreamofcode@gmail.com.

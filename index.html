Vault Blitz: Heist Match

In development · private testing

A match-3 puzzle game about cracking bank vaults. Swap tiles to break safe tumblers, cut through laser locks and clear each vault before your moves run out.

  • A campaign of vaults

    Work through 40 vaults across four districts, from Downtown Financial to the Offshore Syndicate. Each vault can earn up to three stars.

  • Gear for tight spots

    Earn in-game gold by clearing vaults and spend it on one-use tools such as Thermal C4, the EMP Spark and the Master Key.

  • Heist Academy

    Short drills teach special tiles, laser locks and power-up combinations.

Vault Blitz is not yet available in the App Store or on Google Play. It is being tested with a small group of invited testers, and features may change before any public release. Progress is saved on your device; there is no cloud save.

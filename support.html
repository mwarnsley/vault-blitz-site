Support

Found a bug, got stuck on a vault, or have an idea? Email us. Vault Blitz is a small project in testing, so replies are handled as time allows.

  • Email idreamofcode@gmail.com

What to include

The more of this you can tell us, the faster we can find the problem:

  • Your platform and device model, for example “iPhone 15” or “Pixel 8”.
  • The iOS or Android version.
  • The app version and build number, shown at the bottom of Settings → Intel in the game.
  • The vault or screen you were on.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps to make it happen again.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps show the problem.

Never send passwords, access tokens or payment-card details. We will never ask for them.

Sending feedback from a test build

iPhone (TestFlight)

You can send feedback directly through TestFlight: take a screenshot while playing and choose Share Beta Feedback, or open the TestFlight app, select Vault Blitz and choose Send Beta Feedback. TestFlight adds your device and build details for you. You can also email us.

Android (Google Play internal testing)

Email us at idreamofcode@gmail.com with the details listed above.

About your progress

Your progress is saved on your device. The game has no cloud save of its own, so we cannot look up, recover or restore it. On Android, the operating system may back up or transfer eligible app data depending on your device and backup settings, but restoration is not guaranteed. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or using Reset Local Progress or Delete Account in Settings can erase it.

Privacy

Test builds send crash and error reports to help us fix problems. See the Privacy Policy for what they contain, and how we handle emails you send us.
